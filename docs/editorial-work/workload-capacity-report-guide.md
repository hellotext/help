## Reader task and article-specific plan

The reader needs to distinguish work performed during the chosen period, the percentage of capacity consumed, and live queue signals before changing Inbox capacity or staffing.

1. Correct **Manejadas / Handled**: count distinct conversations with a qualifying human handling interval overlapping the chosen period, not first replies. Replies, internal notes and human closes can start handling during an active session. One conversation can appear in more than one daily bucket or teammate/team row, so do not use the Handled minus Resolved gap as a backlog count.
2. Correct **Carga activa / Active load** to a percentage of recorded handling time over available session capacity. Explain its relationship to Capacity pressure, and clarify that **Concurrencia / Avg. concurrent** averages simultaneous handling during active work. Use one short numeric session example to distinguish capacity, active time and concurrency; it is illustrative, not a product result.
3. Clarify **Resueltas / Resolved** as human resolution, and **Asignadas / Assigned** and **Transferidas a / Transferred to** as handling overlapping the selected period under an ownership interval. Assigned means no handoff from another teammate for that interval; the teammate need not be the first human owner ever, and assignment/transfer can predate the chosen period.

Correct **Presión operativa / Operational Pressure**: Unanswered, Oldest waiting and SLA risk inspect current unresolved response obligations, while Utilization and Concurrent use selected-period work; Burn combines both.
