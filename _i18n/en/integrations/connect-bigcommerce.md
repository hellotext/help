Connect BigCommerce so Hellotext can bring in customers, products, orders, and storefront activity, and show popups, webchat, and the WhatsApp widget in your store.

There is nothing to install on a server, and you never copy keys or code.

## What the integration syncs

- Customers and newsletter subscribers
- Products, variants, prices, images, categories, and stock
- Orders and their status: placed, paid, shipped, delivered, cancelled, and refunded
- Product views and cart activity in your store

## Before you connect

Confirm that:

- You are the store owner, or your BigCommerce user has the **Install applications** permission.
- Your store uses a BigCommerce theme. Custom-built storefronts sync customers, products, and orders, but do not get browser tracking or widgets.
- You are using a regular browser window. Private windows can block the installation.

## Install Hellotext

1. In your BigCommerce control panel, go to **Apps** and find **Hellotext** in the marketplace.
2. Select **Get This App**, choose your store, and review the permissions.
3. Confirm the installation. Hellotext opens inside your control panel.
4. Select **Continue in Hellotext**. A new tab opens.
5. Sign in or create your account, and choose the business to connect.
6. If your BigCommerce account has several online stores, choose one. Orders from the others do not reach Hellotext.
Products, recent orders, and our code in your store are set up automatically.

To bring in your existing customers, go to **Settings > Integrations**, open the menu next to your store, and select **Import Customers**.

## Customers and consent

Importing customers or receiving an order does not subscribe anyone to your messages. People who accepted your store's newsletter are added to the **BigCommerce newsletter** list so you can segment them.

## Cookie banner

If your store shows the BigCommerce cookie banner, each part of Hellotext loads only when the shopper accepts its category:

| Category | What loads |
| --- | --- |
| Functional | Webchat and the WhatsApp widget |
| Targeting | Popups and Push notifications |
| Analytics | Visit and cart activity, which also starts messages such as the cart reminder |

Showing the banner, and complying with the privacy rules that apply to your store, is your responsibility. Without a banner, everything loads.

## Good to know

- **Delivered orders:** BigCommerce has no delivered status. Hellotext treats **Completed** orders as delivered, so delivery messages depend on your team using that status.
- **Cart reminders for guests:** BigCommerce reports an abandoned cart after at least one hour without changes.
- **Products that require a choice:** products with a required option, such as an engraving, cannot go in a cart recovery link.
- **Changing your account email:** if the email of your BigCommerce account changes, open the Hellotext app again to authorize it.

## Check the connection

Open **Apps > My Apps > Hellotext** in BigCommerce. The page shows whether the connection is healthy, how many updates are active, and when data last arrived. You can share that page with Support without giving access to your store.

In Hellotext, go to **Settings > Integrations**. If BigCommerce stops accepting the connection, the store is marked as needing authorization.

## Disconnect

Remove the integration from **Settings > Integrations**, or uninstall the app from **Apps > My Apps**. Our code leaves your store and BigCommerce stops sending data. Your orders and contacts stay in Hellotext.
